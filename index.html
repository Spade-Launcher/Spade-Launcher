FAQ

What is Spade Launcher? +

Spade Launcher is a minimalist home screen launcher for Android. It replaces your phone's cluttered home screen with a calm one: clock, weather, your favorite apps, and fast search. It helps you use your phone with intention instead of getting pulled in.

Is Spade Launcher free? +

Yes. Spade Launcher is free on Google Play with no ads. Ace Edition is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks extra customization. It's not a subscription.

Which phones does Spade Launcher work on? +

Any Android phone running Android 7.0 or newer, including Samsung, Google Pixel, Xiaomi, OnePlus, and Motorola. The app is available in 19 languages.

Can Spade Launcher help me reduce screen time? +

That's what it's built for. There are no notification badges or feeds on the home screen, you can hide distracting apps from the app drawer, and Focus Gate lets you set a time limit on a chosen app and blocks it once the time is up.

Does Spade Launcher collect my data? +

No personal data is collected. When purchasing Ace Edition, a cryptographic receipt issued by Google Play is sent to a self-hosted server to verify the purchase — no personal information is included. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I switch back to my old launcher? +

Yes, anytime. Just change your default home app in Android Settings. No hoops.

What is Ace Edition? +

It's the premium version of the launcher with more customization, widgets, Themes, App Icons available, as well as the possibility to add your own icon packs and so on.

Are there ads? +

No. Never will be.
